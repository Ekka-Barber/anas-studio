## PART 1 — Anas: Deep Psychological & Brand Profile

### 1.1 Snapshot
Anas Abdullah Al-Qarni is an **engineer who chose the world of warmth** — coffee, hospitality, memory, and writing — and refuses to pick a lane between them. He is not a "founder showing off projects." He is a **quiet builder and a memory-keeper** who measures his life in *traces left behind*, not logos owned. The brief he wrote is the single best window into him: every section name is a small poem, every closing line reverses expectation, and the section he is proudest of ("مرّت من هنا") is the one where *his name does not appear on anything*.

### 1.2 The core archetype — الصانع الحكّاء (The Craftsman-Storyteller)
A blend of three classic drives:
- **The Creator** — "كل شيء يبدأ بفكرة." Ideas are his raw material; he is idea-first in everything, even jam ("حين تتحول القصة إلى منتج").
- **The Sage / Memory-Keeper** — خوص, the book, "بعضها يعيش في الذاكرة." He preserves what is quietly disappearing. خوص (woven palm frond) is not décor for him — it is heritage, the grandmother's hands, the vanishing Saudi home. "محاولة لحفظ ما لا يجب أن يضيع."
- **The Caregiver / Host** — ذرى, "الكرم السعودي," hospitality reimagined. He builds *experiences that make people feel held*.

### 1.3 The central duality (this is the whole design problem)
> **Engineer (precision · systems · structure) ⟷ Storyteller (warmth · memory · soul).**

He has not resolved this by choosing — he has resolved it by **integrating**, and he explicitly wants the site to *be* that integration ("هوية واحدة واضحة"). The design must feel **engineered and warm at once**: a precise grid holding soft, literary, hand-touched content. Think *museum architecture wrapped around a family home*. This single tension is the north star — every type choice, every layout, every photo should sit on this seam.

### 1.4 Core drives & values (with design implications)
| Drive | Evidence in his words | What it means for the site |
|---|---|---|
| **Legacy over ego** | "بعضهم يبنون أثراً يعيش داخلها" · "ليست كل البصمات تحتاج إلى توقيع" | No "look at me." His name is small; the *work and the stories* are large. Anti-hero layout. |
| **Impact over ownership** | Proudest of "مرّت من هنا," where he owns nothing | The contributions section must be the emotional peak, not an afterthought. |
| **Narrative-first** | Even jam is "قصة تتحول إلى منتج" | Every project is introduced with a *line of meaning before any data/specs*. |
| **Patience / ripening** | "ما زال على الرف… بعضها يحتاج فقط إلى الوقت المناسب" | He proudly shows *unfinished* work. The site should feel unhurried, with breathing room and slow reveals. |
| **Gratitude & loyalty** | Names his teacher: "من ثقة معلمي الشيخ حامد السهيمي" | Mentors/family are honored visibly, warmly — not as credentials but as relationships. |
| **Heritage → contemporary** | ذرى = "الضيافة السعودية بصورة معاصرة" | Saudi roots, modern restraint. Never folkloric kitsch; never cold startup minimalism. |

### 1.5 Emotional register & relationship to time
Calm, reverent, nostalgic-but-forward. He writes in **classical literary Arabic with restraint** — short lines, ellipses (…), reversal-of-expectation closings — *exactly* the taste that rejects "جميع الحقوق محفوظة" in favor of a poem. His relationship to time is **slow and ripening**: ideas have a right season. The site should never feel like it's selling or rushing. It should feel like *walking slowly through a warm house at dusk*.

### 1.6 What he will LOVE vs. what he will REJECT
**Will love:** generous whitespace · warm cream/earth palette · beautiful classical-modern Arabic typography · paper / خوص-weave / coffee / palm textures · slow scroll reveals · a quiet, museum-like calm · the poetry of the small detail · his literary lines given room to breathe.

**Will reject (do NOT do this):** bright blue or neon · saturated gradients · startup "disruption" language · loud CTAs ("اطلب الآن!") · English-corporate speak · stock-photo gloss · busy Islamic-geometric wallpaper · exclamation marks · heavy diacritization · anything that screams "personal brand influencer."

## PART 2 — Visual System (design tokens)

### 2.1 Color palette (IG-confirmed: jewel tones on cream)
| Token | Hex | Role |
|---|---|---|
| `paper` (cream) | `#F4ECE0` | Primary background (matches ذرى highlight) |
| `sand` | `#E7D8C3` | Secondary surface / cards |
| `ink` | `#1C1A17` | Primary text / headlines (matches Friends highlight) |
| `forest` ★ | `#234A30` | **PRIMARY accent** — nature/greenery (Family-project highlight) |
| `plum` | `#4A2740` | Secondary literary accent (Mates highlight) |
| `midnight` | `#20223F` | Depth / footer / contact (تواصل معنا highlight) |
| `oud` (coffee brown) | `#6E4B33` | Warm tertiary / RAHHA tie-in |
| `mutedText` | `#6F5E4E` | Body subtext, captions |
| `brass` | `#B28E5A` | Metallic restraint accent / hairline glow |
| `clay` (terracotta) | `#B65C43` | Optional warm spark only — use sparingly |
| `line` | `#D9C9B4` | Hairline rules, borders |

Rule: **cream base, always — never white or grey.** Then **one deep jewel accent per screen**: `forest` is the default (hero, primary links); rotate `plum` or `midnight` for variety between sections so each room has its own quiet mood. `brass` for metal details. Keep `clay` as a rare spark, not a theme.

### 2.2 Typography (the duality, expressed in type)
- **Display / literary headlines → `Amiri`** (classical naskh, the *storyteller*). Use for the hero line, section poems, book excerpts. ~10% tashkeel max, never more.
- **Hero flourish (optional, one place only) → `Aref Ruqaa`** for the name أنس عبدالله القرني.
- **Body / UI / navigation → `IBM Plex Sans Arabic`** (clean, modern, the *engineer*). Alternatives: `Tajawal` or `Almarai`.
- Concept: classical type carries meaning; modern sans carries structure — the engineer⟷storyteller seam, made literal.
- RTL throughout. Generous `line-height` (1.9–2.1 for Arabic body). Large type scale; let headlines be big and lines short.

### 2.3 Texture & imagery direction
Subtle paper grain on backgrounds · faint خوص-weave or linen texture in section dividers · photography that is warm, shallow-depth, natural-light, *quiet* (coffee detail, hands, palm shadow, clay, brass دلة). No glossy product-shot lighting. Generous negative space around every image.

### 2.4 Motion principles
Slow, soft, intentional. Fade-and-rise on scroll (16–24px, 600–800ms, ease-out). No bounce, no parallax circus. One gentle reveal per section. The site should feel like it *exhales*.

## PART 6 — Design execution & handoff (what was built)

> Added July 2026. The Part 3 prompt was run through Claude Design and exported as a handoff bundle (`anas-abdullah-al-qarni-s-digital-home/project/`). This part records **what the finished design actually is** — which in several places is richer than, or diverges from, the original brief. The design is the reference the coding build recreates pixel‑for‑pixel.

### 6.1 What's in the handoff bundle
- **`Home.dc.html`** — the full one‑scroll homepage (hero → three circles → all room previews → contact → footer).
- **Dedicated room pages** — `بدأت هنا`, `بُنيت هنا`, `مرّت من هنا`, `كُتبت هنا` (book), `على الرف`, `المشاهد`, `تواصل`.
- **`Style Board.dc.html`** — the built design system (palette, type pairing, links/buttons, cards, form fields, texture/dividers). Every value is coded and ready to lift.
- **`Motion Spec.dc.html`** — one motion language: easing `cubic-bezier(.22,.61,.36,1)`, 700–900ms reveals, 16–24px rise, one reveal per section, reversible up/down, plus a full "Tweaks" control map and a `reduce‑motion` respect switch.
- **`Responsive Frames.dc.html`** — mobile (375) + tablet (768) frames for hero, the peak, the book, and the gallery.
- **Fonts** — self‑hosted **Lyon Arabic Display** (OTF) + **Thmanyah Sans/Serif** (woff2), with the licensed Lyon family also under `uploads/`.
- **`support.js`** — the Claude Design runtime (custom `x-dc` element + `DCLogic`); *not* shipped — the coding build re‑implements the behaviour in Astro.

### 6.2 What the finished design LOCKED (vs. the original brief's "OR" choices)
- **Typography upgraded.** Headlines/literary = **Lyon Arabic Display** (not Amiri — Amiri is now only the fallback). Body/UI = **Thmanyah Sans** (IBM Plex Sans Arabic is the fallback). Name flourish falls back to Aref Ruqaa. Thmanyah Serif is available for editorial. Client **holds the web licence** for Lyon + Thmanyah, so they self‑host as designed.
- **Palette confirmed** exactly as Part 2 (cream + jewel tones): paper `#F4ECE0`, sand `#E7D8C3`, ink `#1C1A17`, **forest `#234A30` (primary)**, plum `#4A2740`, midnight `#20223F`, oud `#6E4B33`, muted `#6F5E4E`, brass `#B28E5A`, clay `#B65C43`, hairline `#D9C9B4`.
- **Room → accent mapping is now explicit:** بدأت=forest · بُنيت=**midnight** · مرّت=plum · على الرف=oud · book/scenes/contact=forest. (Note: بُنيت's accent resolved to *midnight*, matching the "تواصل معنا" navy from his IG highlights.)
- **Numbers are always Latin digits;** no exclamation marks; light tashkeel only — all as briefed.

### 6.3 What the finished design ADDED or CHANGED (scope deltas — read these)
- **The book became a microsite with commerce.** `كُتبت هنا` is no longer a quiet "قريباً + one email field." It now has: anchored sub‑nav (نبذة · شخصيات · مقتطفات · صور · رحلة الكتاب), a **custom RTL 3D page‑flip reader**, **three purchasable editions** (رقمية 45 · ورقية 90 · موقّعة 150 ر.س), **add‑to‑cart + a slide‑in cart drawer + إتمام الطلب**, *and* a **notify‑me** email capture. This introduces **payments (SAR), an orders schema, and fulfilment/refund obligations** that the original brief never scoped. → treated as a **V2 workstream** in the PRD; V1 shows editions + notify‑me only.
- **The risky interactions were NOT used.** No horizontal‑scroll section, no drag‑the‑sketches (على الرف is **static tilt cards**), no character‑by‑character hero animation (the hero reveals **word‑by‑word**, keeping Arabic letter‑joining intact). مرّت من هنا uses **always‑visible plaque cards**, not desktop‑only hover‑reveal. This deletes most of the grilling sheet's "interactions that don't survive mobile" round.
- **The gallery** (المَشَاهِد) is CSS multi‑column masonry with a quiet filter row + hover caption; lightbox still to be chosen at build.
- **بُنيت stat row** shows `14` فروع · `60+` منتج/حملة · `9` سنوات — **illustrative numbers pending Anas's greenlight** (they publish counts of a real business).

### 6.4 The through‑line held
The design honours the emotional brief: **مرّت من هنا** is given the most air, the largest type on the site, and the fading **أنس** signature watermark that literally enacts "ليست كل البصمات تحتاج إلى توقيع." The pitch's winning frame is built into the product.

## PART 7 — Locked build decisions (ready for coding)

> The full engineering spec is **`anasaq-me-prd-v1.md`**. This is the one‑screen summary. The remaining *content/product* questions (audience, circle rules, plain project descriptions, brand rights, PDPL, and the four new book‑commerce questions) are tracked in **`anasaq-prd-grilling.html`** and are the only things blocking a content‑complete V1.

| Area | Decision |
|---|---|
| Framework | **Astro** — static output + islands (zero JS by default; RTL‑native) |
| Host | **Cloudflare Pages** (static) + **Pages Functions** for form/API endpoints |
| Database | **Supabase** free tier + a **heartbeat cron** (prevents 7‑day auto‑pause) |
| Images | **Cloudflare R2** (10 GB free, zero egress) for gallery + book art |
| Fonts | Self‑host **Lyon Arabic Display** + **Thmanyah Sans/Serif** (licence held); fallbacks Amiri / Aref Ruqaa / IBM Plex |
| Animation | Vanilla CSS + `IntersectionObserver` (as handoff). No Aceternity / Magic UI / Framer / Lenis |
| Spam | **Cloudflare Turnstile** + honeypot on every public form |
| Email | **Resend** (contact + notify + order emails) |
| Analytics | **Cloudflare Web Analytics** (cookieless, no consent banner) |
| Payments (V2) | **Moyasar** (mada/Apple Pay/cards, hosted page) or Tap — needs a merchant account |
| i18n / a11y | Arabic‑only V1 (`dir=rtl`); WCAG 2.2 AA; full reduced‑motion fallback |
| Perf budget | LCP < 2.5s / initial < 1 MB / JS < 150 KB gz / Lighthouse ≥ 90 |

**MVP (V1):** home one‑scroll + all rooms + contact + book (reader preview + editions display + notify‑me) + gallery + على الرف. **V2:** book checkout/payments, gallery admin CMS, optional English.

**Why this stack:** the finished design is content‑first and mostly static, so Astro on Cloudflare Pages ships it fast at ~$0 and **removes the entire "tech stack contradicts itself" problem** the grilling sheet opened with (no Next‑on‑Cloudflare beta, no Server‑Components conflict, no LTR‑only effect libraries fighting Arabic).
