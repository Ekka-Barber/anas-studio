# Product

## Register

brand

## Platform

web

## Users

Visitors to Anas Abdullah Al-Qarni's digital home (anas.studio), in Arabic, mostly on phones: people who know his food and hospitality work in Tabuk, partners weighing a project with him, readers waiting for his book «خوص | حكايات شارع 4», and future customers of his store. The admin (`/admin`) serves a second audience: Anas and his staff editing their own words, posts, catalogue and orders; it follows the product register inside the same palette.

## Product Purpose

A home for the ideas, places and stories he has built, in his own words: the rooms where things started, were built, passed through, wait on the shelf, and were written. It shows his work truthfully, lets people reach him, and will sell his book and products when he opens the store. Success is a visitor who understands who he is from his own voice and knows how to take the next step: read, buy, or write to him.

## Positioning

«أبني الأفكار والأماكن والحكايات»: not a portfolio of clients, but the places that carry something of him, told in the first person.

## Brand Personality

Warm, crafted, proud without boasting. His palette (sand, coral, aubergine, with saffron from the book) woven into bands like palm leaf (khous), the Najdi triangle of his ذرى card, his handwritten signature. Arabic first, right to left, poster-sized display type with calm reading text.

## Anti-references

The frozen v1 "cream museum" and invented statistics (the v2 study); SaaS templates and hero-metric blocks; generic editorial-magazine layouts; stock imagery standing in for his own photographs; decorative placeholders.

## Design Principles

- His words, exactly: texts come from Anas and the CMS; the site typesets, never rewrites.
- Truthful states: nothing shown that does not work or does not exist yet.
- One woven world: every page is built from the same bands, edges and roles (DESIGN.md).
- Readable first: content is visible without motion or JavaScript; motion only adds.
- Easy to edit: tokens, tones and roles in one place; composition in small, named components.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA: text contrast measured per tone (DESIGN.md), visible focus in each band's colour, 44px targets, a skip link, native dialogs for the menu and the lightbox, labelled forms with errors in words, reduced motion honoured everywhere, films never autoplay, Latin digits in Arabic text.
