How to review

  1. Go through the screens and mark each one Approve or Needs a change.
  2. When something should change, say what and where in the note. Arabic or English both work.
  3. Click a shot to open it at full size. In that view, press B and A to flip between before and after at the same place.

Your marks are saved with this page, and I read them from here.

What changed

  • A night rail at the start of a wide screen (from 1280 px), a bar on a phone, a tablet and a small laptop: the mark, «أنس» and the nav in four groups, in the order every role already had.
  • The current page is a sand tab cut out of the rail, laid over the palm-weave strip.
  • Screen titles in Thmanyah Serif Display after the three-triangle mark, closed by a 3 px rule; sections are paper panels.
  • Fields and buttons are 46 px tall, square, with 2 px aubergine lines; every link and control is at least a 44 px target.
  • Tables sit in a paper box with a sand header row; at 480 px and below each row becomes a labelled card (the short version history stays a table, with smaller buttons).
  • The owner home flows in two columns, the content home is a set of doors, and the library keeps the chosen picture beside the grid.
  • Pages are longer: about 26% on a phone and 16% on a laptop, because controls grew to 46 px, sections became panels and fact lists became ruled rows (the paid order as operations sees it, at 360 px: 3,786 to 5,486 px). If you want denser screens, say so on any screen and I tighten the spacing everywhere at once.

What was checked

  • No page scrolls sideways: 62 screens at six widths from 360 to 1440 (372 views). Tables fit from 1024 up and become cards at 480 and below; only at a tablet's 768 do the orders list and the mail scroll inside their own box (the orders list by 87 px, where the old one scrolled 328 px).
  • On a phone and a tablet the nav strip keeps the current page's link whole in view on every screen, also after a resize, a page change, a role change or a late font, and fades only toward links that are out of view.
  • The rail fits every laptop window down to 1280 x 600, the current page's tab covers the weave, and every focus ring sits inside its box, also under real Tab presses.
  • tasmeem's scan: 0 findings; its in-page checks: what remains is by design, older than the redesign, one rule misreading a one-word button, or the harness's own stand-in.
  • impeccable's detector: 0 findings.
  • Every text colour pair is 4.5:1 or more (aubergine on the fields' paper: 9.5:1).
  • Lint, typecheck and 2,583 unit tests pass. An Opus audit compared every screen's structure with the old admin's: nothing the screens do changed. Two Opus audits failed earlier states of the redesign; every finding was fixed.
