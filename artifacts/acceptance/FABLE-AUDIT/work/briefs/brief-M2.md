M2-1 (ADMIN-COMMERCE-04, a defect introduced by this audit's round M1a-5). public.order_resolve (final body in 20261007100000_fable_audit_payments.sql) refuses PAYMENT_REVERSED when `v_attempt.provider_status in ('refunded', 'voided')`. Moyasar sets a payment's status to 'refunded' after a PARTIAL refund too (docs.moyasar.com/docs/guides/payment-operations, «Partial Refund»: 'The payment status changes to refunded'), so after the owner refunds the one missing line, as the STOCK_UNAVAILABLE message tells him to, resolve becomes impossible. Fix: decide by money and by the one status that means a full reversal: `if v_attempt.provider_status = 'voided' or v_attempt.provider_refunded_halalas >= v_attempt.captured_halalas then PAYMENT_REVERSED`. Tests (order-operations.test.ts): the partial case 'A part refunded at the provider is no reversal' must model what Moyasar produces: `status: 'refunded', refunded: total - 1` and still resolve; a full refund (refunded = total, status 'refunded') and a void stay PAYMENT_REVERSED.
