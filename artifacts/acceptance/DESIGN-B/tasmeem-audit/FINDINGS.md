## What works and must be kept

- **Direction B carries every public page at every width.** The bands in the seven tones, the crenels and the weave are there throughout, with poster-sized Thmanyah display over calm reading text (18–21px at 2.05). No page falls to a template silhouette: no centred hero, no icon cards, no stat strip, no generic footer. The home's first screen is the name bands and the portrait; the footer is his poem and his signature.
- **Truthful states:** the journal's empty state, sections hidden while their material is missing, demo products labelled «(تجريبي)», prices shown only from data.
- **Arabic correctness, measured on every route:** `lang="ar" dir="rtl"`; zero letter-spacing, italic, `text-transform` or faux bold on Arabic; Western digits only (no Arabic-Indic digit anywhere); every Latin token (anas.studio, @anasa.aq, emails and phones in inputs) isolated with `dir="ltr"`. «←» means forward and «→» back; the play triangle is not mirrored. The signature writes left to right because it is Latin («Anas Alqarni»).
- **No horizontal overflow** at any width (render at four widths, plus a probe for content hidden by `overflow-x: clip`: none).
- **Dialogs:** the menu and the lightbox are native modal `<dialog>`s. Focus moves in, Escape closes, focus returns to the opener, `aria-expanded` toggles, the page scroll is locked and then released, and the lightbox's arrow keys follow RTL («←» is next).
- **Motion:** CSS entrances that always finish, reveals held only below the fold, and a small vocabulary with one job per effect (band wipes in the reading direction, media wipes upward, the signature writing itself). The motion-on runs found no held content, and under reduced motion there are zero animations and no loops.
- **Films:** `preload="none"`, no bytes before the tap, muted, never autoplayed, native controls once playing.
- **The book's section navigation:** `scroll-margin` clears the sticky bar, and `aria-current` follows the reading position.
- **The contact form** is the model for the rest: a visible label on each field, errors in words (coral-deep, 4.85:1), `aria-invalid` and `aria-describedby`, focus on the first invalid field, and `dir="ltr"`, `inputmode` and `autocomplete` on email.
- **Book imagery:** cover B and the standing mockup were checked letter by letter («خوص», «حكايات شارع 4», «أنس عبدالله القرني»), with the spine on the right as an Arabic book binds. The photographs are Anas's own.
