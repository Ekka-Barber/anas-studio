## Audit 1: audit_failed

### R6-A1 [medium] supabase/migrations/20261002130000_refunds.sql:745
- Problem: refund_record_external reopens the most recent NOT_APPLIED refund with its own allocation without checking that the allocation still fits. If the owner retried the same item after the first refund was declared NOT_APPLIED and the first one then lands late, the item ends with more allocated than it cost. finance.item_fully_refunded (payment_core.sql:482) tests equality, so an item that was fully refunded stops counting as refunded. Round 7b's ITEM_REFUNDED refusal, to_ship filter and order_resolve all rely on that function, so a twice-refunded physical item would become shippable again. This is the retry-after-timeout case of invariant 4, and it breaks the contract rule that an item's refunded total stays within line_subtotal minus discount. The worker listed it as an open risk and left it unfixed.
- Evidence: Probe on the local database in one rolled-back transaction (scratchpad audit-probe.cjs), on an order with a 1500-halala digital item: R1 (item, 1500) aged 16 minutes and settled at total 0 -> failed NOT_APPLIED; R2 (same item, new key) -> new, refund_result succeeded at 1500, item_fully_refunded = true; next request with provider total 3000 -> PROVIDER_AHEAD; refund_record_external(3000) -> {ok:true, amount:1500, refundId = R1} (reopened). Afterwards: item paid 1500, allocated by succeeded refunds 3000, item_fully_refunded = false, order still paid. Lines 732-751 never call finance.refund_allocation_ok. No test covers it: refunds.test.ts:1317-1374 reopen only when nothing else was refunded in between.
- Correction: Needs a contract ruling, because section 6 says the refund is reopened "with its own allocation". Recommended, inside the allowlisted migration: before reopening v_late, require finance.refund_allocation_ok(v_order_id, p_attempt, v_delta, v_late.allocation) (v_late is still failed, so it is not counted); when it no longer fits, leave v_late failed and record the delta as an unallocated provider_dashboard refund. Alternative ruling: create-or-replace finance.item_fully_refunded with >= and restated revokes. Either way add a refunds.test.ts case: NOT_APPLIED refund of item B, a second refund of B succeeds, the first lands and is recorded; assert B's allocated total is at most what it cost and item_fully_refunded(B) stays true.

### R6-A3 [low] supabase/migrations/20261002130000_refunds.sql:581
