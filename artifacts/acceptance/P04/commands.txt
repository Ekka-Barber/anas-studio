## Open blocker for the orchestrator
tests/unit/content.test.ts is not in my P04-part-1 write allowlist. It predates
this package and calls src/lib/content.ts's getters synchronously
(`getStartedRoom().heroLine`), which this package's required change (async
loaders reading published_documents via the Data API, per the P04 brief) made
incompatible: both `pnpm typecheck` and `pnpm build`'s type-check step fail on
it, and 4 of its own test cases throw at runtime. It needs either an async
rewrite (small, mirrors tests/integration/content.test.ts's pattern) or
removal now that tests/integration/content.test.ts covers the same getters
against a real database. This blocks `pnpm build` from completing; every other
proof step passed.
